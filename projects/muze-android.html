Android Music Player "MUZE Player" Open Source Project

We have released first beta version of music player for Android called - Muze Player. Development goals are to make music player that is as simple and easy to use, as well as visually appealing and with essential futures. Another redesign phase has started. You can check it and join development efforts on Google Code.
