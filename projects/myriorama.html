MYRIORAMA sonic journey...

MYRIORAMA IS A SONIC JOURNEY GRADUALLY DEVELOPING IN THE FORM OF AN AUDIO COLLAGE. THE SOUNDRACK OF THE COMPOSITION IS BASED ON THE DREAM LOGIC, COALESCENCE BETWEEN DIFFERENT SOUNDSCAPES, SLOW AND MURKY TEMPO, ORGANIC AND SYNTHETIC SOUNDS. THE NARRATIVE CREATED FOR SALOME LAMAS MOVIE "TRIAL BY FIRE" PERFORMED IN CONTEMPORARY ART CENTRE DURING SCANORAMA FILM FESTIVAL COULD BE TREATED AS THE ORIGIN AND THE SOUNDRACK SOURCE OF INSPIRATION FOR THE PIECE.

Authors: Vladas Dieninis and Gintautas Rožė and code Stanislovas Mickus and design Tadas Karpanavičius.
